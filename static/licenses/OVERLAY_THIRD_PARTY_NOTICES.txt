Sample manga pages (Settings -> Help screenshots and store listing images)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Title:  Give My Regards to Black Jack   (Japanese title: ブラックジャックによろしく)
Author: SHUHO SATO                      (佐藤秀峰)

The manga artwork, cover images, and Japanese dialogue reproduced in the Help
screenshots and the store listing images come from the complete-edition volume
data that Sato Manga Production (有限会社佐藤漫画製作所) and Shuho Sato publish for
free secondary use at https://densho810.com/free/ . Those terms allow
reproduction, public transmission, and adaptation for both commercial and
non-commercial purposes without prior consent, on condition that the title and
author name above are displayed for each work; every screenshot therefore shows
both. Only the publisher's own distributed data may be redistributed - never a
reproduction of the printed book - so every page shown in this app is rendered
from those official files; see scripts/fetch-help-manga-fixture.mjs. The
permission covers the original thirteen-volume series only: the sequel "Shin
Black Jack ni Yoroshiku" and Sato's other works are not included.

The English dialogue shown in the translated-overlay screenshots is taken from
the publisher's own English edition of volume 1, distributed from the same page.
